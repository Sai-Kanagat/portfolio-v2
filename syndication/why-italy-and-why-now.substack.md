# Why Italy, and Why Now

I get asked this every time someone reads my CV. India to Italy is a long way. Industrial design to digital marketing is a long way too. Doing both at once looks, on paper, like a person who can't make up his mind.

It isn't. Both moves are the same move.

## The unfinished argument

Industrial design taught me that objects carry meaning. Marketing taught me that meaning carries markets. The pivot was less a career change than the discovery that I'd been studying half a discipline.

I'm doing this in Italy because Italy is one of the few countries where the integration between design culture and commercial identity is treated as a national competence. You cannot understand Ferrari, Olivetti, Alessi, Vespa, or Brunello Cucinelli without understanding both axes simultaneously. The brand *is* the design. The design *is* the brand.

## What I'm doing here

Bologna Business School, MSc in Digital Marketing & Communication. Bologna over Milan was deliberate. Milan is where the campaigns happen. Bologna is where the thinking that produces good campaigns happens — smaller, denser, more honest relationship to craft.

The programme covers the standard stack, but the texture is different. A constant low-grade insistence on history, taste, and the durability of brand decisions. That's the Italian inheritance, and it's exactly what most digital marketing ecosystems are missing.

## What I'm looking for

A full-time role in Italy after the programme. Marketing or brand strategy. Ideally somewhere that doesn't treat content, paid media, brand and product as four separate departments speaking four separate languages. The work I'm best at sits at the joint between them.

## The bigger reason

I think Italy is undervalued. It exports brand culture better than any other country in Europe and gets relatively little of the Anglophone marketing-tech industry's attention. That's an arbitrage. There's a generation of operators here who can think both axes — design and distribution — and the international market is hungry for exactly that combination.

I want to spend the next decade being one of them.
