Building
Nishta

Nishta is the digital marketing practice I founded in January 2024. It covers social media advertising, web design, branding and content strategy, and its first clients were BikeWo and Vijay Gas Service.

Where it came from

I was working as a designer in India in 2023. I kept noticing that the gap between good craft and good commerce in small Indian brands was almost entirely a marketing gap: beautiful products, terrible distribution, no brand language. The people making the things were not the people selling the things, and the people selling the things weren't designers. So the products felt designed and the storefronts felt accidental.

Nishta was my attempt to close that gap from the marketing side. Design discipline on the brand and how it communicates, marketing discipline on the results. One person, both halves of the brief.

What I'd do differently

I'd ship uglier earlier. I spent too long polishing my first versions because I was the only designer in the room and nobody was pushing me to stop.

I'd also separate the founder identity from the brand identity earlier. For the first nine months Nishta and I were the same brand in my head, and it muddied both. Brands need their own voice, even when they start as one person's project.

Why this matters for what I do now

Nishta is the reason I take marketing roles seriously. If you've ever been the person who writes the ads, briefs the photographer and answers the client email, all in the same week, you don't talk about marketing as a "function" anymore. You talk about it as the thing that decides whether the work gets results.
