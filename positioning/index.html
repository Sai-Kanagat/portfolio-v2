Positioning Mapper

Paste each competitor's homepage headline, tagline and price. The page scores them on five axes from the copy, shows its reasoning, lets you overrule any number, and maps two axes at a time. Then it finds the crowded zones and the white space, drafts a positioning statement and lists the claims everyone makes versus the claims nobody makes. Nothing leaves your browser.
