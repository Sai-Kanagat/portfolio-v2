Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

I am Sai Kanagat, based in Bologna, Italy, and I run this site. Questions or requests: saikanagat1117@gmail.com.

What happens before you choose

In Europe, nothing is sent to analytics until you press Accept. While the notice is open, this page notes what happens in it (for example that you opened it and clicked something) in your browser's memory only. If you accept, those notes are sent together with everything after. If you decline, or leave without choosing, they are discarded and never sent. They are not kept from page to page.

Outside Europe, the site counts visits from the start and shows a notice where you can opt out. A Global Privacy Control signal counts as a decline everywhere.

Whatever you choose, the page loads fonts and code libraries from Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr and Google's file storage. The Aperol microsite asks sunrise-sunset.org for sunset times for a city chosen on the page, not your location. These companies receive your IP address, as any web server does. Cloudflare hosts the site and processes your IP address to deliver it.

What is counted if you accept

  • Pages opened, time on them, how far you scrolled, which parts were in view
  • Clicks on buttons and links, and cursor position as a coarse grid
  • Which demos and tools you used, and steps such as "camera allowed" or "PDF exported"
  • Form steps such as started, error or sent, never what you wrote
  • Your country, type of device and language, the site you came from, and the short code of the link you used. A code stands for a post or an application. I never name a code after a person
  • A random visitor ID and visit ID kept in your browser

What is never collected by analytics

What you type, files or file names, camera or microphone content, hand or face data. The camera and microphone demos process everything in your browser. My own store does not keep IP addresses.

Who processes it

  • Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager. Ads features and Google Signals are off.
  • Microsoft Clarity records visits as replays and heatmaps, with all text masked. It uses cookies.
  • My own store on Cloudflare, in western Europe, holds the same events.
  • Formspree receives what you send through the contact form, because you asked me to reply.
  • Brevo receives your email if you subscribe to the newsletter.
  • Cortex sends the brand files or text you give it to its own server, only to run that tool.

Some of these companies are in the United States.

How long it is kept

  • My store: 14 months. Cursor grid: 90 days
  • Google Analytics: 14 months
  • Clarity: replays 30 days, with a small sample kept up to 9 months. Heatmaps 9 months

Your choices

Use "Privacy choices" at the bottom of a page at any time. Turning it off stops collection, removes the analytics cookies and IDs from this browser, and sends nothing more. Write to me to see, correct or delete data about you. My store holds only a random ID, so I may need that ID from your browser to find it. You can also complain to the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Legal basis: your consent in Europe for analytics. For messages and newsletters, your request.
