Drop a scenario.
Watch a crowd think it through.

Agora is a sandbox for rehearsing public reaction. Type in a question, statement, or scenario — a product launch, a policy proposal, a news event. The engine generates a synthetic crowd of personas, runs them through a multi-tick simulation of conversation and influence, then hands you a prediction report.

Lite Version This is a stripped-down browser demo. The full version uses real LLM-driven agents with persistent memory, GraphRAG-backed personas extracted from the input material, dual-platform parallel simulation, and a ReportAgent that lets you chat with any persona post-run. Get in touch to see it.
