— 00 · principlessix rules, on repeat.

design things that sell.
Every artefact has a job — convert, convince, communicate. Beauty is a means, never the brief.
both sides of the brief.
Industrial craft + digital strategy. Lead with one, back it with the other. Never abandon either.
made to last.
Editorial structure, generous whitespace, real grids. Decisions you'd defend in five years.
built to grow.
Performance > perfection. If a tweak earns better CTR, ship it. Measure everything.
one accent, used loudly.
Ultramarine carries every signal. Used sparingly it punches; sprinkled around it whispers.
one italic per surface.
Newsreader italic is the system's smile. Always one true italic per surface — never both feet on the design floor.
