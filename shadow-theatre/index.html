How to make each shadow

Hold a shape still for about a second and the creature steps off the wall. Keep your hands a forearm's length from the camera, side on to it.

Dog

Hand side on, fingers pointing sideways and held together as the snout. Lift your thumb up as the ear. Drop your little finger for an open jaw.

Bird

Both hands, palms toward you. Hook your thumbs together in the middle and spread the fingers of each hand wide like wings.

Rabbit

Point your index and middle fingers straight up in a V for the ears. Fold your ring and little fingers down onto your thumb.

Duck

Hand side on. Bring every fingertip together onto your thumb to make a closed beak, then point it sideways.

Shadow
theatre.

Hold your hands up to the candle. Make a dog, a bird, a rabbit or a duck, keep it still for a second, and watch it step off the wall and come alive. Anything else still casts a shadow, with a little stardust.

The camera is only requested after you press start. Every frame is processed locally in your browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
