How to make each shadow

Hold a shape still for about a second and the creature steps off the wall. Many of these come from the hastas of Bharatanatyam, the hand gestures a dancer uses to draw a swan, a deer or a lotus in the air. Keep your hands a forearm's length from the camera.

Shadow
theatre.

Hold your hands up to the candle. Thirty shapes live here, many borrowed from the hastas of Bharatanatyam: a swan, a deer, a lion, a lotus, a crescent moon. Hold one still for a second and watch it step off the wall and come alive. Anything else still casts a shadow, with a little stardust.
