Four jobs, one system.

  1. 01

    It writes for every channel

    One brief becomes the post, the email, the ad copy and the caption, each written for its channel and in the brand’s own voice.

  2. 02

    It designs every asset size

    Each piece comes out in every size a channel asks for, built from the brand’s templates, so nobody crops by hand.

  3. 03

    It audits what is live

    Technical SEO, visibility in AI search, broken links and brand voice, checked against what is actually published.

  4. 04

    It measures against forecasts

    A forecast is sealed before a piece ships. At day two and day seven the real numbers are scored against it, misses included.
