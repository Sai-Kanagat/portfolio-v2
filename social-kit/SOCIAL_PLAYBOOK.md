## 7. Google Search Console — DO THIS TODAY

2. Add property → **URL prefix** → enter `https://saikanagat.pages.dev/`
3. Verification: pick **HTML tag** method → copy the meta tag they give you (looks like `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="xxxx..." />`)

One real backlink from any of these = weeks of organic-only SEO progress, compressed into a day.
